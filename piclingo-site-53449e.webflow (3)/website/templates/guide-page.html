How to write a better prompt?

🎨 Be Specific:
Clearly describe what you want in your image. Specify scenes, objects, and characters to avoid confusion.
‍
🖌️ Include Style Preferences:
Mention the artistic style or mood you're looking for. Choose from options like realistic, surreal, abstract, or impressionist.
‍
🌈 Provide Constraints:
Set specific parameters such as color palette, time of day, or any other details that matter to you. This guides the AI's creative process.
‍
🔄 Experiment with Multiple Prompts:
Try out different versions of your prompt to discover varied interpretations. Experimentation can lead to unique and interesting results.
